Discover Triund Trek

Welcome to the breathtaking Triund Trek, one of the most popular and scenic treks in Himachal Pradesh. Nestled in the lap of the Dhauladhar range, this trek offers mesmerizing views of snow-capped peaks and lush valleys. Starting from McLeodganj or Dharamkot, the trail takes you through rhododendron forests, chirping birds, and panoramic landscapes that feel straight out of a postcard.

The Triund Hill stands as a peaceful escape, offering stunning sunsets and a starlit night sky that trekkers cherish forever. Known as the "Crown Jewel of Dharamshala," it’s a beginner-friendly trek yet rewards you with 360° views of the Kangra Valley and mighty Himalayan ranges.

Triund is the perfect destination for those seeking adventure, serenity, and a short getaway. Whether you’re a solo traveler, nature enthusiast, or adventure seeker, this trek promises memories to last a lifetime.

Highlights of Triund Trek

  • Panoramic Views: Majestic Dhauladhar ranges on one side and the vast Kangra Valley on the other.
  • Beginner-Friendly: A perfect trek for first-timers, with well-marked trails and moderate difficulty.
  • Camping Experience: Spend the night under a sky full of stars at Triund Hill.
  • Flora & Fauna: Walk through oak, deodar, and rhododendron forests with diverse bird species.
  • Short Getaway: Can be completed in 1–2 days, making it a quick yet rewarding trek.

Experiences at Triund

  • Trekking: A 9 km trail from McLeodganj/Dharamkot that takes 4–6 hours.
  • Camping: Experience peaceful nights with bonfires and starry skies.
  • Photography: Sunrise and sunset views make Triund a photographer’s paradise.
  • Local Culture: Explore Tibetan cafés in McLeodganj before or after your trek.

How to Reach Triund

The trek to Triund begins from McLeodganj or Dharamkot, both well-connected by road from Delhi and Chandigarh. The nearest airport is Kangra Airport (Gaggal), about 18 km from Dharamshala. The nearest railway station is Pathankot, from where buses and taxis are easily available. Regular buses operate between Dharamshala and major North Indian cities.

FAQ'S

What is the best time to trek Triund? ▼
The best time to visit Triund is from March to June and September to November. Winters are snow-covered and adventurous, but trekking becomes harder.
Is Triund suitable for beginners? ▼
Yes! Triund is considered a beginner-friendly trek with moderate difficulty, ideal for first-time trekkers.
Can we camp at Triund? ▼
Absolutely! Camping at Triund under the stars is the most popular activity, offering a magical experience.
How long is the Triund Trek? ▼
The trek is around 9 km (one way) and usually takes 4–6 hours depending on pace.
Do we need a guide for Triund? ▼
A guide is not mandatory as the trail is well-marked, but beginners can hire one for safety and local insights.
