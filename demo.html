FAQs

What is included in the trek cost?➕

Professional trek lead, support staff, camping gear, permits/fees, meals as per itinerary, and basic first‑aid. Transport to base can be added.

Is it safe for beginners?➕

Yes. Start with an easy trek (Triund/Kheerganga). We do a quick fitness & gear check before departure.

Best time to trek in Himachal?➕

Spring (Apr–Jun) and Autumn (Sep–Oct) suit most trails. High passes open mid‑Jun to Sep depending on snow.

What to pack?➕

Trekking shoes, 30–40L backpack, 2‑3 warm layers, rain jacket, cap, gloves, water bottle, headlamp, and basic toiletries. A detailed checklist is shared after booking.
