Lake Explorer Trek | 7 Days 6 Nights

Embark on an extraordinary journey to one of Himachal Pradesh's most remote and pristine lakes. Lam Dal Lake, hidden deep in the Chamba wilderness, offers crystal-clear alpine waters and untouched mountain landscapes that few have experienced.

What to Expect

Lam Dal Lake is a hidden gem located in the remote wilderness of Chamba district at an altitude of 4,200 meters. This challenging trek takes you through dense forests, high-altitude meadows, and rocky terrain to reach one of the most pristine alpine lakes in the Himalayas. The lake's crystal-clear waters reflect the surrounding peaks, creating a mirror-like surface that changes colors with the light throughout the day.

Wilderness Highlights
  • Pristine Lam Dal Lake at 4,200 meters
  • Remote wilderness camping experience
  • Crystal-clear alpine lake reflections
  • Untouched mountain landscapes
  • Diverse high-altitude flora and fauna
  • Complete solitude and wilderness immersion
  • Challenging high-altitude trekking

Detailed Trek Itinerary

Day 1: Chamba to Base Camp

Start from Chamba town. Drive to trek starting point and establish base camp. Equipment check, acclimatization, and preparation for wilderness journey ahead.

Days 2-3: Forest Trek to High Camp

Trek through dense oak and deodar forests. Gradual ascent through varied terrain with river crossings. Reach high-altitude camp with spectacular mountain views and wildlife spotting opportunities.

Days 4-5: Alpine Meadows to Lam Dal Lake

Cross high-altitude meadows with alpine flowers. Final challenging ascent to Lam Dal Lake. Spend extended time at the pristine lake with photography and exploration. Wilderness camping beside the crystal-clear waters.

Days 6-7: Return Journey

Sunrise at the lake with spectacular mountain reflections. Begin descent through alternative routes. Celebrate successful wilderness adventure and return to Chamba.
