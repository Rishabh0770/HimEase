Weekend Explorer Trek | 2 Days 1 Night

Perfect introduction to Himalayan trekking with the mystical Prashar Lake. Experience the floating island phenomenon and enjoy panoramic mountain views in this beginner-friendly adventure.

What to Expect

Easy trail through beautiful rhododendron and oak forests leading to the sacred Prashar Lake at 2,730m. The lake features a unique floating island that changes position and an ancient pagoda-style temple dedicated to Rishi Prashar. Enjoy stunning 360-degree views of Dhauladhar, Kinnaur Kailash, and Pir Panjal ranges.

Perfect For
  • First-time trekkers and families
  • Weekend warriors seeking quick getaway
  • Nature lovers and photographers
  • Spiritual seekers and temple enthusiasts
  • Those wanting easy Himalayan experience

Detailed Trek Itinerary

Day 1: Kullu to Prashar Lake

Morning (8:00 AM): Pick-up from Kullu/Manali. Drive to Baggi village (1 hour). Begin easy trek through dense forests and meadows.

Afternoon (2:00 PM): Reach Prashar Lake. Explore the floating island and ancient temple. Set up camps with stunning lake views.

Evening (6:00 PM): Enjoy sunset views, photography session, and cultural stories around bonfire. Dinner and overnight camping.

Day 2: Prashar Lake to Kullu

Morning (6:00 AM): Wake up to spectacular sunrise over the mountains. Early morning photography and temple visit.

Afternoon (11:00 AM): Trek back to Baggi village. Experience different perspectives of the landscape on descent.

Evening (4:00 PM): Drive back to Kullu/Manali with beautiful memories and photos.
