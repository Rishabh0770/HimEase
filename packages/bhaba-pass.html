Trans-Himalayan Explorer | 8 Days 7 Nights

Experience one of the most dramatic landscape transitions in the Himalayas. Cross the challenging Bhaba Pass and journey from the lush green valleys of Kinnaur to the stark, high-altitude desert of Spiti.

What to Expect

This challenging trans-Himalayan trek offers incredible diversity in landscapes, cultures, and ecosystems. Start in the apple orchards and green valleys of Kinnaur, cross the formidable Bhaba Pass at 4,865m, and emerge into the moonscape terrain of Spiti Valley. Expect technical river crossings, steep ascents, and dramatic weather changes.

Trek Highlights
  • Cross the spectacular Bhaba Pass at 4,865 meters
  • Experience dramatic landscape transformation
  • Journey through Kinnaur's lush valleys to Spiti's desert
  • Technical river crossings and challenging terrain
  • Ancient monasteries and traditional villages
  • Stunning views of Kinnaur Kailash range
  • Unique flora and fauna of two different ecosystems

Detailed Trek Itinerary

Days 1-2: Kafnu to Mulling Base Camp

Start from Kafnu village in Kinnaur. Trek through beautiful pine forests and traditional villages. Establish base camp at Mulling with stunning valley views and acclimatization.

Days 3-4: Mulling to Phustirang

Cross challenging terrain and multiple river crossings. Ascend to Phustirang camp with panoramic mountain views. Prepare for the pass crossing ahead.

Days 5-6: Bhaba Pass Crossing to Spiti

The most challenging section - cross Bhaba Pass at 4,865m. Witness dramatic landscape change from green Kinnaur to stark Spiti. Steep descent into Pin Valley.

Days 7-8: Mud Village and Spiti Exploration

Reach Mud village in Spiti. Explore local monasteries, traditional architecture, and celebrate successful pass crossing. Cultural immersion and departure.
