Kheerganga Classic Trek | 2 Days 1 Night

Experience the magical journey to Kheerganga, the sacred hot springs nestled in the heart of Parvati Valley. This classic trek takes you through dense forests, charming villages, and pristine mountain trails.

What to Expect

Begin your adventure from Kasol, journey through Barshaini village, and trek alongside the roaring Parvati River. Discover hidden waterfalls, cross wooden bridges, and finally reach the mystical hot springs of Kheerganga where you can relax in natural thermal pools under a starlit sky.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Kasol to Kheerganga
  • Morning (8:00 AM): Pick up from Kasol, drive to Barshaini
  • 9:00 AM: Trek briefing and start of Kheerganga trek
  • 10:30 AM: Pass through Rudranag waterfall
  • 12:00 PM: Lunch break at scenic spot
  • 1:00 PM: Continue trek through pine forests
  • 4:00 PM: Reach Kheerganga, check into camps
  • 5:00 PM: Visit sacred hot springs
  • 7:00 PM: Evening snacks and tea
  • 8:30 PM: Dinner and bonfire
  • 10:00 PM: Stargazing and overnight camping
Day 2: Kheerganga to Kasol
  • 6:00 AM: Early morning hot springs bath
  • 7:30 AM: Breakfast and packing
  • 8:30 AM: Start descent to Barshaini
  • 11:00 AM: Reach Barshaini village
  • 11:30 AM: Drive back to Kasol
  • 12:30 PM: Trek completion and drop-off

Package Inclusions
