Pass Adventurer Trek | 6 Days 5 Nights

Experience the thrill of conquering Sach Pass, one of India's most challenging and adventurous mountain passes. Journey from the lush valleys of Chamba to the remote and pristine Pangi Valley through spectacular mountain terrain.

What to Expect

This challenging adventure trek takes you across the formidable Sach Pass at 4,420 meters, known for its unpredictable weather and technical terrain. Experience dramatic landscape changes, challenging river crossings, and some of the most remote and untouched regions of Himachal Pradesh. The route is famous among adventure motorcyclists and trekkers for its difficulty and stunning beauty.

Adventure Highlights
  • Cross the challenging Sach Pass at 4,420 meters
  • Experience remote and untouched Pangi Valley
  • Navigate technical terrain and river crossings
  • Witness stunning Himalayan panoramas
  • Explore traditional villages in Pangi Valley
  • Challenge yourself on one of India's toughest treks
  • Experience the thrill of high-altitude adventure

Detailed Trek Itinerary

Day 1: Chamba to Bairagarh

Start from Chamba town. Drive and trek to Bairagarh base camp. Acclimatization and preparation for the challenging days ahead. Beautiful valley views and traditional village exploration.

Days 2-3: Bairagarh to Satrundi

Trek through challenging terrain with river crossings and steep ascents. Reach Satrundi camp with panoramic mountain views. Prepare for the pass crossing with equipment checks.

Day 4: Sach Pass Crossing

The most challenging day - cross Sach Pass at 4,420m. Navigate technical terrain, possible snow patches, and unpredictable weather. Descent into Pangi Valley with spectacular views.

Days 5-6: Pangi Valley Exploration and Return

Explore remote Pangi Valley villages and culture. Experience the isolation and beauty of this hidden region. Celebrate successful pass crossing and prepare for return journey.
