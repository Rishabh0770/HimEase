Peak Explorer Trek | 3 Days 2 Nights

Embark on an exciting journey to Churdhar Peak, the highest mountain in Sirmaur district. This moderate trek offers stunning panoramic views of the Himalayas and a chance to visit the ancient Shirgul Devta temple at the summit.

What to Expect

Churdhar Peak stands majestically at 3,647 meters and is known for its spiritual significance and breathtaking views. The trek takes you through dense forests, alpine meadows, and traditional mountain villages. The summit hosts an ancient temple dedicated to Shirgul Devta, making it both a physical and spiritual journey. The panoramic views from the top encompass the entire Himalayan range.

Trek Highlights
  • Summit of Churdhar Peak at 3,647 meters
  • Visit ancient Shirgul Devta temple
  • 360-degree panoramic Himalayan views
  • Trek through diverse forest ecosystems
  • Experience local mountain culture
  • Perfect introduction to high-altitude trekking
  • Spiritual and religious significance

Detailed Trek Itinerary

Day 1: Nohradhar to Churdhar Base

Start from Nohradhar village. Trek through beautiful oak and rhododendron forests. Reach Churdhar base camp with spectacular mountain views. Evening campfire and preparation for summit day.

Day 2: Summit Day - Churdhar Peak

Early morning start for summit. Trek to Churdhar Peak (3,647m) via scenic mountain trails. Visit Shirgul Devta temple at summit. Enjoy panoramic Himalayan views. Descent to base camp for overnight stay.

Day 3: Base Camp to Nohradhar

Morning leisure at base camp with mountain views. Descent through forest trails back to Nohradhar. Trek completion ceremony and departure.
