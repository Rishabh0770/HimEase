Hampta Pass Classic Trek | 5 Days 4 Nights

Experience the classic Hampta Pass trek that crosses from lush Kullu Valley to the stark, lunar-like landscape of Lahaul-Spiti. This legendary trail offers dramatic scenery changes and unforgettable high-altitude adventure.

What to Expect

Begin in Manali's green forests, trek through Jobra and Jwara, cross the challenging Hampta Pass at 4,270m, and witness the spectacular transition to Lahaul's desert mountains. Experience camping under star-studded skies and reach the pristine Chandratal Lake.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: Manali to Chika via Jobra
  • Morning (7:00 AM): Pick up from Manali, drive to Jobra (10,100 ft)
  • 10:00 AM: Trek briefing and equipment check
  • 11:00 AM: Start trek to Chika base camp
  • 1:00 PM: Lunch on trail
  • 4:00 PM: Reach Chika campsite (10,800 ft)
  • 6:00 PM: Evening tea and snacks
  • 8:00 PM: Dinner and briefing for next day
  • 9:30 PM: Overnight camping
Day 2: Chika to Balu ka Gera
  • 6:00 AM: Early morning tea and breakfast
  • 7:30 AM: Pack up and start trek
  • 10:00 AM: Cross river streams and boulder sections
  • 1:00 PM: Lunch break at scenic location
  • 4:30 PM: Reach Balu ka Gera campsite (11,900 ft)
  • 6:00 PM: Acclimatization walk
  • 8:00 PM: Dinner and rest
Day 3: Balu ka Gera to Shea Goru via Hampta Pass
  • 5:00 AM: Early wake up, light breakfast
  • 6:00 AM: Start ascent to Hampta Pass
  • 9:00 AM: Reach Hampta Pass summit (14,100 ft)
  • 10:00 AM: Photography and rest at pass
  • 11:00 AM: Start descent to Shea Goru
  • 2:00 PM: Packed lunch on descent
  • 5:00 PM: Reach Shea Goru campsite (11,500 ft)
  • 8:00 PM: Celebration dinner
Day 4: Shea Goru to Chandratal Lake
  • 7:00 AM: Breakfast and packing
  • 8:30 AM: Trek to Chatru (11,000 ft)
  • 11:00 AM: Drive from Chatru to Chandratal
  • 1:00 PM: Lunch en route
  • 4:00 PM: Reach Chandratal Lake (14,100 ft)
  • 5:00 PM: Explore Moon Lake and photography
  • 7:30 PM: Dinner by the lake
  • 9:00 PM: Stargazing and overnight camping
Day 5: Chandratal to Manali
  • 6:00 AM: Sunrise photography at lake
  • 7:30 AM: Breakfast and packing
  • 9:00 AM: Start drive back to Manali
  • 1:00 PM: Lunch at Keylong/Koksar
  • 6:00 PM: Reach Manali, trek completion
