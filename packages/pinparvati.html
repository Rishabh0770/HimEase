Pin Parvati Pass Expedition | 10 Days 9 Nights

Embark on one of India's most challenging and rewarding treks. The Pin Parvati Pass expedition takes you through pristine wilderness, crossing the formidable pass at 5,319m between Parvati and Pin valleys.

What to Expect

This expedition traverses remote wilderness areas with challenging terrain including river crossings, glacier travel, and technical sections. Experience dramatic landscape transitions from lush Parvati Valley to the stark, high-altitude desert of Pin Valley in Spiti. Requires advanced trekking experience and technical skills.

Expedition Requirements
  • Minimum 5 high-altitude treks completed
  • Technical mountaineering experience
  • Excellent physical fitness
  • Previous glacier travel experience
  • Valid mountaineering course certificate

Detailed Expedition Itinerary

Days 1-3: Kasol to Tunda Bhuj Base Camp

Journey from Kasol through Barshaini to Tunda Bhuj. Gradual acclimatization and equipment checks. Pass through dense forests and establish base camp.

Days 4-6: Tunda Bhuj to Mantalai Lake

Trek through challenging terrain to reach the sacred Mantalai Lake. Technical river crossings and steep ascents. Advanced camping at high altitude.

Days 7-8: Pin Parvati Pass Crossing

The most challenging section involving glacier travel, rope work, and technical climbing. Cross the formidable Pin Parvati Pass at 5,319m altitude.

Days 9-10: Descent to Pin Valley and Mud Village

Dramatic descent into Pin Valley's stark landscape. Reach Mud Village in Spiti and celebrate expedition completion with local cultural exchange.
