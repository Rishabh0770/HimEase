Places Covered

Delhi » Shimla » Sarahan » Sangla » Chitkul » Tabo » Kaza » Key Monastery » Chicham Bridge » Langza » Hikkim » Komic » Chandratal » Manali » Delhi

Experience the ultimate Himalayan adventure with our "Spiti 4x4 Expedition" — a complete Spiti Circuit covering the best of Kinnaur and Spiti Valley. Starting from Delhi, journey through Shimla, Sarahan, Sangla, Chitkul — the last village of India — then deep into Spiti via Tabo, Kaza, Key Monastery, Chicham Bridge, and the world's highest villages of Langza, Hikkim & Komic. End this epic expedition with a night camping at the magical Chandratal Lake before returning via Manali to Delhi. All transfers in premium 4x4 SUVs with an experienced Trip Captain throughout.

What To Expect!

In this 6 Nights / 7 Days Spiti 4x4 Expedition, expect raw Himalayan wilderness, ancient monasteries perched on cliffs, surreal desert landscapes, and stargazing skies unlike anything you've seen. Cross high-altitude passes, visit living fossils in Langza, send a postcard from the world's highest post office at Hikkim, and camp by the ethereal Chandratal Lake. With Breakfast & Dinner included, a dedicated Trip Captain, and premium 4x4 vehicles — this is Spiti done right.

This 6 Nights / 7 Days itinerary takes you on a complete Spiti circuit — from Delhi through Kinnaur, deep into Spiti, and back via Manali. Every day is a new landscape, a new story.

Day-by-Day Itinerary

DAY 1 — DELHI TO SHIMLA → SARAHAN▼
  • Depart from Delhi early morning.
  • Drive through Narkanda and Rampur towards Sarahan.
  • Visit the famous Bhimakali Temple in Sarahan.
  • Unwind by the bonfire in the evening.
  • Dinner and overnight stay at Sarahan.
DAY 2 — SARAHAN → KALPA → SANGLA → CHITKUL▼
  • Early morning departure from Sarahan / Kalpa.
  • Enjoy morning views of Kinner Kailash peak.
  • Drive to Chitkul — the last village of India on the Indo-Tibet border.
  • Return to Sangla for evening check-in at homestay.
  • Overnight stay at Sangla.
DAY 3 — SANGLA → NAKO → TABO▼
  • Early morning departure from Sangla.
  • En route visit Nako Lake and Nako Monastery.
  • Continue journey to Tabo, home of the 1000-year-old monastery.
  • Evening check-in at homestay.
  • Overnight stay at Tabo.
DAY 4 — TABO → DHANKAR → KAZA▼
  • Visit the ancient Tabo Monastery in the morning.
  • Drive towards Kaza, stopping at the cliffside Dhankar Monastery.
  • Cross the scenic Pin Valley Bridge with stunning views.
  • Stop at Lingti Waterfall (scenic en route).
  • Overnight stay at Kaza.
DAY 5 — KAZA LOCAL SIGHTSEEING▼
  • Wake up early and have breakfast.
  • Visit iconic Key Monastery.
  • Cross the thrilling Chicham Bridge (Asia's highest gorge bridge).
  • Visit Langza (Fossil Village), Hikkim (World's Highest Post Office) and Komic (World's Highest Village).
  • Return to Kaza — enjoy stargazing under the Milky Way at night.
  • Overnight stay at Kaza.
DAY 6 — KAZA → KUNZUM PASS → CHANDRATAL LAKE (CAMPING)▼
  • Early morning departure from Kaza.
  • Drive via Kunzum Pass (4,590m) — prayers and panoramic views.
  • Arrive at campsite — check-in and freshen up.
  • Visit the magical Chandratal Lake — leisure time by the turquoise lakeside.
  • Dinner at campsite followed by overnight stay under the stars.
DAY 7 — CHANDRATAL → MANALI → DELHI (DEPARTURE)▼
  • Early morning breakfast at campsite and check-out.
  • Scenic drive via Rohtang Pass / Atal Tunnel to Manali.
  • Explore Manali local market for some time.
  • Evening board the bus for an overnight drive to Delhi.
  • Reach Delhi by next morning — trip ends with memories of a lifetime!
