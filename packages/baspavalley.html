Valley Explorer Trek | 5 Days 4 Nights

Immerse yourself in the enchanting Baspa Valley, known for its pristine river, ancient villages, and rich Kinnauri culture. This moderate trek combines natural beauty with cultural exploration in one of Himachal's most beautiful valleys.

What to Expect

Baspa Valley, located in the Sangla region of Kinnaur district, is renowned for its stunning natural beauty and rich cultural heritage. The valley follows the pristine Baspa River and is dotted with traditional Kinnauri villages, ancient temples, and lush apple orchards. Experience the unique blend of Hindu and Buddhist cultures that characterize this region, along with spectacular views of snow-capped peaks and dense deodar forests.

Cultural Highlights
  • Explore traditional Kinnauri villages and architecture
  • Visit ancient Buddhist monasteries and Hindu temples
  • Experience local Kinnauri culture and hospitality
  • Walk through famous apple orchards and farms
  • Enjoy pristine Baspa River and mountain landscapes
  • Learn about traditional Kinnauri crafts and lifestyle
  • Taste authentic Kinnauri cuisine and local products

Detailed Trek Itinerary

Day 1: Sangla to Chitkul

Start from Sangla town, drive and trek to Chitkul - the last inhabited village near Indo-Tibet border. Explore village culture, visit local monastery, and acclimatization with Baspa River views.

Day 2: Chitkul Valley Exploration

Full day exploring Chitkul and surrounding areas. Visit traditional houses, interact with locals, explore apple orchards, and enjoy mountain photography with spectacular valley views.

Day 3: Chitkul to Rakcham

Trek through beautiful valley landscapes to Rakcham village. Experience traditional Kinnauri architecture, visit local temples, and enjoy riverside camping with mountain views.

Day 4: Rakcham to Sangla

Explore Rakcham village and surroundings. Visit Kamru Fort, experience local culture, and return to Sangla through scenic valley routes with cultural stops.

Day 5: Sangla Cultural Tour and Departure

Explore Sangla town, visit Bering Nag Temple, local markets, and cultural sites. Cultural immersion activities and departure with valley memories.
