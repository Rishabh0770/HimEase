High-Altitude Explorer Trek | 6 Days 5 Nights

Embark on an incredible journey through Spiti's high-altitude desert landscape to reach the mesmerizing Chandratal Lake, also known as the Moon Lake, situated at 4,300 meters above sea level.

What to Expect

This challenging trek takes you through some of the most spectacular high-altitude desert landscapes in the Indian Himalayas. Experience dramatic terrain changes, pristine alpine lakes, and the raw beauty of Spiti Valley. The trail offers stunning views of snow-capped peaks and the unique ecosystem of the cold desert.

Best For
  • Experienced trekkers seeking high-altitude adventure
  • Photography enthusiasts and landscape lovers
  • Adventure seekers wanting unique terrain experience
  • Those interested in Spiti's cold desert ecosystem
  • Trekkers comfortable with challenging conditions

Detailed Trek Itinerary

Day 1: Manali to Baralacha Pass

Early morning departure from Manali. Drive through Rohtang Pass and Keylong to reach Baralacha Pass (4,890m). Acclimatization and setup base camp. Stunning views of surrounding peaks.

Day 2: Baralacha Pass to Suraj Tal

Trek to the beautiful Suraj Tal (Sun Lake) at 4,950m. Experience high-altitude terrain and pristine lake views. Camp near the lake with spectacular sunset views.

Days 3-4: Suraj Tal to Chandratal Lake

Cross challenging high-altitude terrain to reach the stunning Chandratal Lake. Set up camp and explore the area. Photography sessions during golden hours.

Days 5-6: Chandratal to Spiti and Return

Explore Chandratal surroundings, visit nearby attractions in Spiti Valley. Begin return journey with memories of the stunning Moon Lake.
