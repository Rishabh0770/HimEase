Triund Basic Trek Package | 2 Days 1 Night

Experience the classic Triund trek with our basic package, perfect for first-time trekkers and budget-conscious adventurers. This package includes all essentials for a safe and memorable trek to the jewel crown of Dharamshala.

What to Expect

Start your journey from McLeod Ganj, the spiritual home of the Dalai Lama. Trek through dense rhododendron forests and reach the stunning Triund meadows. Enjoy panoramic views of Dhauladhar ranges and spend a night under the stars. This easy to moderate trek is perfect for beginners and offers breathtaking sunrise and sunset views.

Detailed Itinerary

Day 1: McLeod Ganj to Triund
  • Morning (9:00 AM): Meet at McLeod Ganj bus stand, brief introduction and trek briefing
  • 9:30 AM: Start trek from Gallu Devi Temple via Dharamkot
  • 11:00 AM: Reach Dharamkot village, short rest and refreshments
  • 12:00 PM: Continue trek through magical forest trail
  • 2:00 PM: Lunch break at scenic viewpoint
  • 4:00 PM: Reach Triund base, set up camps
  • 6:00 PM: Evening snacks and tea
  • 7:30 PM: Dinner and bonfire (weather permitting)
  • 9:00 PM: Stargazing and overnight camping
Day 2: Triund to McLeod Ganj
  • 5:30 AM: Wake up for magical sunrise views
  • 7:00 AM: Morning tea and light breakfast
  • 8:00 AM: Pack up and start descent
  • 10:00 AM: Reach Dharamkot, refreshment stop
  • 12:00 PM: Arrive at McLeod Ganj
  • 12:30 PM: Trek completion, certificate distribution
